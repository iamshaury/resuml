# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students (specifically engineers) and active LinkedIn users.

## Product Purpose
A simple, low-friction platform to build tailored resumes in real-time and track job applications. Success means a fast, unbloated, and delightful experience.

## Positioning
Real-time resume builder with zero complex setup, designed specifically for the fast-paced workflow of student engineers applying to jobs via platforms like LinkedIn.

## Operating Context
Users are actively in the stressful phase of job hunting, likely importing jobs from LinkedIn and tailoring their resumes on the fly. The tool must feel effortless.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must remain incredibly simple to use.
- Core capability: Real-time resume builder.
- Constraint: Avoid feature bloat and visual clutter.

## Brand Commitments
- Aesthetic: Bright, playful colors (moving away from generic/bland UI).
- UI Philosophy: Minimalist but vibrant.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real-time resume builder interface.
- Application tracking Kanban board.
- Chrome extension for LinkedIn integration.

## Product Principles
1. **Low friction:** Zero complex setup; get straight to building.
2. **Focus on action:** The real-time resume builder is the hero feature.
3. **Clarity through reduction:** Remove extra elements and unused buttons that don't serve the core workflow.
